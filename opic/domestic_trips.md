### 1-1: You indicated in the survey that you take vacations domestically. Tell me about some of the places you like to travel toand why you like going there.
I really enjoy traveling within Vietnam because there are so many beautiful places to explore. Among them, Cat Ba Island is one of my favorite destinations.

I like Cat Ba because it has a great combination of beaches, mountains, and the sea. The water is clear, and the scenery around Lan Ha Bay is really beautiful. I especially enjoy taking a boat tour because I can see many small islands and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere.

Another thing I like about Cat Ba is that there are many activities to do. I can go swimming, explore Cat Ba National Park, or simply relax on the beach. After working for a long time, I really enjoy being surrounded by nature.

That's why Cat Ba is one of the places I want to visit again. Every time I go there, I feel relaxed and refreshed.

### 1-2: Can you tell me about the things you do in order to prepare for trips?
Whenever I plan a trip, I usually prepare a few days in advance because I don't want to forget anything important.

First, I decide where I want to go and check the weather. Then I book my transportation and hotel. If I'm going to Cat Ba, for example, I usually check the ferry schedule and find a hotel that is close to the beach.

After that, I pack my clothes based on the weather and the activities I plan to do. For a beach trip, I usually bring comfortable clothes, swimwear, sunscreen, and a pair of sandals. I also make sure I have my ID, wallet, phone charger, and some medicine.

Before I leave home, I check everything one more time. I also look up interesting places and restaurants online.

I know it takes some time to prepare, but good preparation makes the trip much more comfortable and less stressful.

### 1-3: Traveling can lead to many kinds of interesting,funny and unexpected experiences. Tell me about one travel experience you had that was unforgettable. Start by telling me when this happened, where you were,and who you were with. And then, tell me about all the things connection that happened that made this experience so memorable.
One of my most unforgettable travel experiences happened last summer when I went to Cat Ba Island with one of my close friends.

We were really excited because it was our first time taking a boat tour around Lan Ha Bay. The weather was great, and everything was going perfectly at first.

However, about halfway through the trip, the boat suddenly stopped because there was a problem with the engine. At first, we were a little nervous because we were far from the shore and didn't know how long it would take to fix the problem.

After a few minutes, the tour guide told us that another boat was coming to help us. Since we had nothing else to do, we decided to make the best of the situation. We jumped into the water, swam around the boat, and took some pictures.

About thirty minutes later, another boat arrived and took us back safely.

Looking back, it was actually the most memorable part of the trip. We were nervous at first, but in the end, we laughed about the whole situation. It taught me that sometimes unexpected problems can turn into great memories.

### 2-3: Tell me about some of the trips that you took in your youth. Where did you go? Who did you go with? And what did you do or see during those early trips?
When I was younger, I didn't have many opportunities to travel, so I still remember some of my early trips very clearly.

We took a boat around Lan Ha Bay, and I was really impressed by the scenery. I saw many small islands and beautiful mountains rising from the water. We also went swimming, and I remember being excited because I could see small fish in the clear water.

Another thing we did was visit Cat Ba National Park. We walked through the forest and saw many different plants and animals.

What I remember most is the feeling of being together with my family. I didn't care much about the destination at that age. I just enjoyed spending time with them.

That's probably why that trip is still one of my favorite childhood memories.

### 4-1: You indicated in the survey that you take vacations domestically. People say that traveling has become more difficult in the past 5 years. Tell me about the types of changes you have observed while travelingand talk about how these changes have affected travelers.
I think traveling in Vietnam has changed quite a lot over the past five years.

One major change is that traveling has become much more convenient. Nowadays, people can easily book hotels, bus tickets, and activities through their phones. We can also use online maps and travel apps to find restaurants and interesting places.

Another change is that popular destinations have become much more crowded. For example, places like Cat Ba and other famous beaches can get extremely busy during holidays. Because of this, hotels may become more expensive, and people sometimes have to wait longer for transportation or activities.

I think these changes have both good and bad sides. Technology has made traveling easier and helped us save time. However, crowded tourist areas can make the trip less peaceful.

For me, I usually travel during weekdays or avoid major holidays whenever possible. That way, I can enjoy the destination more comfortably.

### 4-2: What are some issues people have regarding trips? What are the main issues or concerns they typically raise or discuss? What causes these concerns? What is being done to address them for the future?
There are several problems that people often worry about when they travel.

One common concern is the cost. During holidays or peak seasons, hotels and transportation can become much more expensive. Because of that, some people have to spend more money than they originally planned.

Another problem is overcrowding. Popular destinations can become extremely busy, especially during national holidays. This can lead to long lines, traffic jams, and crowded beaches. Sometimes people spend more time waiting than actually enjoying their trip.

People are also concerned about the environment. Too many tourists can create more trash and damage natural places if visitors are not careful.

I think there are several ways to deal with these problems. Hotels and transportation companies can provide better services during busy seasons, while local governments can control the number of visitors in some areas. Tourists also need to follow the rules and take care of the environment.

In my opinion, traveling should be enjoyable, but we also need to be responsible travelers.
