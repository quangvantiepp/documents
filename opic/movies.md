### 3-1: You indicated in the survey that you like to watch movies. Could you compare the movies made today to movies you watched while you were growing up? How have movies changed over the years? What are the differences and similarities?

I think movies have changed quite a lot compared to when I was growing up.

When I was younger, I usually watched movies on television or on DVDs. There weren't as many choices, so I often watched whatever was available. The picture and sound quality were also much lower than they are today.

These days, people can watch almost any movie they want through streaming services. The picture quality is much better, and many movies have amazing special effects.

Another difference is that people can watch movies almost anywhere. We don't always need to go to a movie theater or wait for a movie to be shown on TV.

However, I think movies still have one important thing in common. Whether they are old or new, a good movie needs an interesting story and characters that people can connect with.

So, technology has changed the way we watch movies, but I don't think it has changed what makes a movie enjoyable.
